\section{Limitations and outlook}
\label{sec:limitations}

REACH estimates no aleatoric uncertainty; its guarantees concern finitely many cells of a frozen
classifier, hold per budget and rest on the declared mixture family; its real intervals, on one image
family, are wide. The raw
widths are not on a common scale; pinning makes attribution estimable but beats a constant only where
several sources compete, and neither it nor the radius rule beats simple constants at choosing data. REACH is not a drift monitor: under an exploratory in-subspace Gaussian shift,
inclusion is near $0.8$ at drift of one to two ball radii with the incompatibility test silent
(\cref{fig:a4}). Feature-space novelty need not survive the frozen head (\cref{fig:a3}).
