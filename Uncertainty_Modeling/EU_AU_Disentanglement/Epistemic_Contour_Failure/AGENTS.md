# Research documentation requirements

For work in this research folder, every theoretical decision and substantive change in understanding must be recorded explicitly in the relevant experiment's RESULTS.md (or the folder-level RESULTS.md for cross-experiment work).

List the papers actually consulted, with title, authors/year, link, and consulted section where possible. Cite each paper at the specific claim or decision where it is used, and include a short accurately transcribed supporting quotation when applicable. Respect quotation limits. Distinguish local derivations, source-backed claims, hypotheses, numerical evidence, and proved results. Do not invent or retroactively attribute sources.

Record failed checks that materially change experimental choices, assumptions shared with the learner, controls, limitations, and evidence that challenges the working hypothesis. Keep the proposal status current. Scope claims to the evaluated measure, target, model, and information regime.

## Agreed research framework

Use frequentist evaluation as the main framework: fixed clean contour and contamination law, repeated-sample coverage, error, and width. Bayesian experiments 03–04 are retained as historical baselines/sensitivity studies; do not resume a Bayesian-centered program by default. Reference confidence constructions are evaluation controls, not the new epistemic definition reserved for the second paper. Preserve evidence that narrows or contradicts the intended failure claim.
